Find your rhythm

A world of Gummies.
So many ways to play.

Connect just for the fun of it, set up your next big chain or find a place for every color.

At your pace

Free Play

Tap, drag and connect Gummies of the same color. Set up the board and enjoy a huge chain.

Choose your move

Color Focus

You have 18 moves. The focus color keeps changing: use it to score more and keep connecting the other colors.

A place for every color

Sort Gummies

Move them between containers and bring matching colors together. Think through your moves and find a way to sort them all.

Pick up the pace

Gummy Rush

Gummies fall and pile up. Tap the target color or connect chains to make room before the container fills up.
